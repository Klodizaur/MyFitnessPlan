# MyFitnessPlan v2.1.0

Profiles for everyone in the house, an app for Android TV and Fire TV, and plans whose rhythm can change from week to week.

## Highlights

### Profiles

Several people can now share one MyFitnessPlan, each with their own plans, Log, progress, favourites, folders and settings. A **"Who's training?"** screen asks who's there, and tapping your avatar in the header switches.

- When you update, everything you already have becomes **your profile**. A short welcome lets you name it, pick one of six illustrated avatars or upload your own photo, set a PIN, and choose the language and theme.
- **Add someone** from the "Who's training?" screen: copy your setup or start from scratch, and choose which of your plans they get a copy of. Each profile can scan its own videos folder.
- An optional **PIN** keeps a profile to yourself, and never locks anyone out: "Forgot PIN?" on the computer running MyFitnessPlan removes it.
- AI plan building is set up per profile and starts switched off for new profiles.

### Backups you don't have to think about

- Back up **just your profile** (plans, progress, Log, favourites and settings), automatically every day, every 3, 7 or 14 days, into a folder you choose. The newest 5, 10 or 30 are kept.
- Settings shows how many backups are in the folder and how much space they take, opens the folder for you, and warns you if a scheduled backup didn't happen.
- **Restore** into your profile, or as a new profile on a new computer.
- **Back Up Everything...** in the menu bar/tray icon copies the whole app into a dated folder.

### MyFitnessPlan on your TV

A new **Android TV / Fire TV app** finds MyFitnessPlan on your computer over the home network and runs it on the big screen, driven entirely by the remote. The player goes full screen with next and previous video, loop and rest, and the remote's media buttons. Pick your profile and enter your PIN with the remote.

Settings › **Devices** in the desktop app shares MyFitnessPlan on your home network with one switch, with a QR code for phones and tablets.

### Week-by-week rhythms

Besides one repeating cycle (now up to 28 days), a plan's rhythm can be set **week by week**, for up to 12 weeks, in both the plan builder and the AI plan builder. The builder and the plan preview lay the plan out week by week.

### What's new and what you've done

- **Recently added** videos on the Dashboard and in the Library, with the date each was added, plus **Newest** and **Oldest** sorting.
- Videos and plans show **how many times you've completed them**, and plan cards have **View plan** in their menu.
- **Show all videos** on the Library page lists everything in one place, with each video's folder.

### A better Breakdown

- A **tag cloud** of workout types, body parts, equipment and folders.
- The totals follow the period you choose, and you can pick any **custom date range** on a calendar.

## Also new

- On a phone, the settings gear opens Settings over the page you're on and takes you back to exactly where you were. The Settings tabs show an icon with a small label.
- On a tablet, the plan builder's filters scroll on their own.
- A plan you repeat is counted each time you finish it.

## Upgrading

Install over your current version. On first start the app makes a backup of your database, then turns everything you have into your profile and opens the welcome. It's still worth copying `~/Library/Application Support/MyFitnessPlan/data` somewhere safe first, as with any big update.

The TV app is a separate download (`.apk`) for Android TV and Fire TV. It needs MyFitnessPlan 2.1.0 on your computer with **Share on Local Network** switched on.

The macOS installer is not code-signed, so macOS will ask you to confirm on first open.
