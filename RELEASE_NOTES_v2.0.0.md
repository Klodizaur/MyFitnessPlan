# MyFitnessPlan v2.0.0

A complete redesign. Every screen is new, the app is faster to read at a glance, and it works the same on a phone, a tablet and a desktop. Your plans, videos, log and settings carry over untouched.

## Highlights

### A new look, in all seven themes

Dashboard, Library, Plans, the plan builder, the AI plan builder, Calendar, Log, Settings and the Player were all rebuilt on one shared design. Midnight, Sunset, Forest, Pastel Orange, Pastel Pink, Sky Blue and the new-look Watermelon all carry it, and changing the theme in Settings applies straight away.

On a phone the header floats and follows you, with a tab bar at the bottom. On a tablet the top bar tightens up so everything fits. Dialogs are a centred card on desktop and a bottom sheet on a phone, and errors show up as proper pop-ups.

### A player that feels like a player

Your own scrubber, skip back and forward, speed and fullscreen. **Loop** is now a labelled control with a rest countdown between plays. An **Up next** card follows, and a **Today** strip under the video lists everything in the workout, so you can jump to any part. One button marks a part done, and undoes it again.

### Plans you can browse and start properly

- Open any plan for a **week-by-week preview**, in grid or list.
- A start-plan sheet asks for the start date and whether it's your main or extra plan.
- Each plan card has a ⋯ menu: edit, change cover, back up, duplicate, delete.
- **Favourites**: star plans and they gather in their own category.
- Importing is one menu: from a CSV/TSV sheet, or a plan file exported from the app.

### The AI plan builder, made easier

Step by step (or all on one page, as before). You can leave chosen subfolders out, change the length of the workout rhythm, and see the drafted days in a sideways **preview**. Then **save the plan right away**, or open it in the builder first.

### Calendar, rebuilt

The **Day Tape**, **Week** and **Grid** views have all been redone. A tape day with several videos gets a switcher, rest days are clear, and the view you pick is remembered. The Freeze sheet shows which day is the last one frozen and when workouts resume.

### Find and sort videos your way

- **Favourite videos** collect in a Favourites album.
- A **length filter** (from / up to, with quick ranges) is in every filter, and **Shortest first** joins Longest first, in the Library and in the plan builder.
- Folder pages sort **subfolders** (A → Z, most or fewest videos) and **videos** (A → Z, longest or shortest) separately.
- Hover a video for play and info buttons.

## Also new

- The plan builder shows each day's rest days from the rhythm, lets you lengthen or shorten the rhythm, and re-deals your videos when it changes. A custom category becomes a chip once you press Enter.
- The Log has a ⋯ menu on every entry (note, date, edit video, remove), tabs for History and Breakdown on a phone, and a chart coloured to match the theme.
- Deleting a plan asks first.

## Upgrading

Install over your current version. Your data (plans, videos, log, settings) stays exactly where it is and the app updates it automatically on first start. It's worth copying `~/Library/Application Support/MyFitnessPlan/data` somewhere safe first, as with any big update.

The macOS installer is not code-signed, so macOS will ask you to confirm on first open.
